Teacher retention

Sharon's teacher retention averaged 88.2% in 2015 to 2020 and 87.6% in 2021 to 2026. In both periods it ranks 23rd of the 27 districts, above the state average and below the average of the 26 comparison districts. The change between the two periods, 0.6 points, is within the range of changes across the group (from 3.2 points down to 1.4 points up).
